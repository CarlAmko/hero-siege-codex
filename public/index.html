⚡ The 5-Second Triage Rule

When grinding Hell / Satanic Zones, your inventory fills up in minutes. Use this rapid triage formula:

  • 🔴 ALWAYS KEEP & PRICE CHECK: Any Angelic (Gold), Unholy (Purple), Heroic (Teal), Satanic Grade SS, or Tier 3/4 High Runes (Fawn, Flo, Jol, Nju, Sur, Ber, Jah, Zed).
  • 🟡 CONDITIONAL CHECK: Satanic Grade S and Set S pieces. Only price check if 105%+ Quality, Max Sockets, or high rolls on +All Skills / Magic Find.
  • ⚪ INSTANT DUST / PROSPECT: Satanic Grades B, C, D, low set pieces, and non-superior items. Head directly to the Blacksmith and prospect into Satanic Crystal Fragments.

🎲 Satanic Dice & Quality Multipliers

  • 115% Quality (God Roll): 5x to 10x market value multiplier. Even A-tier uniques become valuable at 115%.
  • 110% - 114% Quality: 2x to 4x value multiplier. Highly sought after by endgame min-maxers.
  • 100% - 109% Quality: Standard baseline value for S/SS items.
  • 50% Quality (Corrupted Brick): Locks item quality at 50%. Loses 80-90% of market value.

🎯 Sockets & Base Prerequisites

  • 2-Handed Weapons: Max 6 sockets (1.8x - 2.5x value spike).
  • 1-Handed Weapons, Armors, Helms, Shields: Max 3 sockets.
  • 0 Sockets: Requires Cube socket recipe or socket punch investment, reducing resale value.
  • Runeword Bases: Superior white bases with 15% Enhanced Damage or Defense + max natural sockets are high-value trade goods.
